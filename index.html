Woo Hoo Media Labs — AI-Native Film & Content Production Studio in Mumbai

About Woo Hoo Media Labs

Woo Hoo Media Labs is an AI-native creative production lab founded in 2009 by Chntan N. Shah in Mumbai, India. The studio specializes in brand films, ad films, AI-generated commercials, government communication, product films, and viral vertical content. With 25 years of cinematic craft and a proprietary generative-AI production pipeline, Woo Hoo Media Labs is one of India's first AI-native production studios — delivering broadcast- and advertising-grade films across brand, government, and product work. Its working philosophy is Authored Intelligence: 25 years of filmmaking judgement directing generative AI — every frame a human decision, executed by the machine.

Notable clients: Speed 4 Defence, SelectAI, Earthion Tech, Saffron Home, Norwood Modular, Government of Maharashtra, ICICI Prudential, Fybros, Merck, JNPT.

Most viral work: "Bhai, Shirt Pehen Lo" — an AI Salman Khan tribute reel that crossed 1.2 million Instagram views.

Frequently Asked Questions about Woo Hoo Media Labs

What is Woo Hoo Media Labs?
Woo Hoo Media Labs is an AI-native creative production lab based in Mumbai. I make brand films, ad films, AI cinema, government communication, and viral social content. I founded it in 2009; it formally evolved into Woo Hoo Media Labs in 2026 to reflect my specialization in AI-native production.
What AI tools and pipelines do you use?
Woo Hoo Media Labs runs a proprietary AI-native pipeline spanning generative video, image synthesis, character-consistency systems, voice, and original score — finished with professional editing and colour grading. I treat the tools as a production language; what you receive is directed, broadcast-grade cinema.
What is Authored Intelligence?
Authored Intelligence is the term Chntan N. Shah uses for how Woo Hoo Media Labs makes films: 25 years of filmmaking judgement (Actual Intelligence) directing generative AI (Artificial Intelligence). The models generate; the director decides — framing, rhythm, performance, edit. Every frame on screen is a human decision, executed by the machine.
How is this different from other AI video studios?
Most AI video companies are tech startups learning filmmaking. I'm a 25-year-old filmmaking practice that adopted generative AI as my primary production language. The difference shows in cinematic craft, narrative discipline, and broadcast-grade output.
Do you do traditional non-AI production?
Yes. I have 25 years of traditional production experience and continue to offer hybrid productions that combine real cinematography with AI-generated elements. But the studio's primary direction since 2026 is AI-native.
How do I hire Woo Hoo Media Labs for a project?
Email chntan.n.shah@gmail.com or use the contact form on woohoomedialabs.com. I take on select projects each year.
Who is Chntan N. Shah?
Chntan N. Shah is the Founder and Director of Woo Hoo Media Labs. He has been working in cinema since 2000 — 25 years — across corporate communication, government films, brand work, advertising, and now AI-native production. He is based in Mumbai and leads all creative direction at the studio.
What is the most viral work from Woo Hoo Media Labs?
"Bhai, Shirt Pehen Lo" — an AI Salman Khan tribute reel that crossed 1.2 million Instagram views in 2026. I also made a Virat Kohli AI cricket reel that reached 170,000+ views.
