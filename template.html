About Woo Hoo Media Labs

Woo Hoo Media Labs is an AI-native creative production lab founded in 2009 by Chntan N. Shah in Mumbai, India. The studio specializes in brand films, ad films, AI-generated commercials, government communication, product films, and viral vertical content. With 26 years of cinematic craft and a generative AI production pipeline including Kling, Seedance, Flux, Nano Banana, and Soul characters, Woo Hoo Media Labs is one of India's first AI-native production studios.

Notable clients: Speed 4 Defence, SelectAI, Earthion Tech, Saffron Home, Norwood Modular, Government of Maharashtra, ICICI Prudential, Fybros, Merck, JNPT.

Most viral work: "Bhai, Shirt Pehen Lo" — AI Salman Khan tribute reel with 1.2 million Instagram views.

Frequently Asked Questions about Woo Hoo Media Labs

What is Woo Hoo Media Labs?
Woo Hoo Media Labs is an AI-native creative production lab based in Mumbai. We make brand films, ad films, AI cinema, government communication, and viral social content. Founded by Chntan N. Shah in 2009, formally evolved into Woo Hoo Media Labs in 2026 to reflect our specialization in AI-native production.
What AI tools and pipelines do you use?
Our generative pipeline includes Kling 2.5/3, Seedance 2.0, Flux 2.0/2.1 Pro, Nano Banana 2, Midjourney v7, Higgsfield, and Soul character models. Voice work uses ElevenLabs. Music uses Suno. Edit and color in Adobe Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve.
How is this different from other AI video studios?
Most AI video companies are tech startups learning filmmaking. We're a 26-year-old filmmaking practice that adopted generative AI as our primary production language. The difference shows in cinematic craft, narrative discipline, and broadcast-grade output.
Do you do traditional non-AI production?
Yes. We have 26 years of traditional production experience and continue to offer hybrid productions that combine real cinematography with AI-generated elements. But the studio's primary direction since 2026 is AI-native.
How do I hire Woo Hoo Media Labs for a project?
Email {{EMAIL_DIRECT}} or use the contact form on woohoomedialabs.com. We take on select projects each year.
Who is Chntan N. Shah?
Chntan N. Shah is the Founder and Director of Woo Hoo Media Labs. He has been working in cinema since 2000 — 26 years — across corporate communication, government films, brand work, advertising, and now AI-native production. He is based in Mumbai and leads all creative direction at the studio.
